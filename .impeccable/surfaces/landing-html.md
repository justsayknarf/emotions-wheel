# Surface brief: landing.html

## Scope
Marketing landing page. Mode: Persuade. Inherits DESIGN.md (The Night Sky Journal) unchanged. Separate Vite entry; the app stays the site root.

## Audience, job, action
Curious first-timers who want to notice how they feel but lack the words. Primary action: open the app ("Start a check-in"). The CTA is a single swappable slot: it may later become sign-up, add-the-extension, or download. Tone: soft, modern, wellness, Oura-adjacent; the brief pins DESIGN.md as the visual authority.

## Proof and content
Real captures of the shipped app (field, pin card, diary, replay) plus the mechanism demonstrated live. No testimonials, usage numbers, or benchmarks exist; none may be invented. Any demonstration data is labeled as sample.

## Constraints
No wrong-answers voice; nothing implying scoring. No social or clinical framing. Honors prefers-reduced-motion. Phone width must hold.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The page is one sky that fills as you scroll; each section adds a pin, and the diary reveals they were a constellation all along. Refuses the stacked feature-card SaaS scaffold and the wellness stock-photo hero.
OWN-WORLD: Night Ink ground with the slow ambient gradient, ember-gold pins, slate for anything already recorded, Bone text in three tiers, Palatino for feeling-words and headlines, Inter for chrome, hairline 1px lines joining points, frosted cards only over live sky. No shadows at rest.
STORY: The visitor sees that a check-in is a single point placed in seconds, that words are optional and never wrong, and that the points add up to a picture of themselves. They open the app.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed sky. Palatino headline left of center, one lede line and one outlined "Start a check-in" beneath it, the same action top right. One gold pin right of the headline at mid-height with the two nearest words (grounded, awe) surfacing beside it, faint field dots and resting words behind. No image frame, no card. On phones the pin sits above a bottom-anchored headline.
FORM: Constellation grows, position 6 of 7 in my ranked list. Seed key 7a310e15.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
